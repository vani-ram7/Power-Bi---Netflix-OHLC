Methodology

Moving-average window

A rolling mean smooths day-to-day noise. This dashboard exposes two windows, 20 and 50 trading days.

MAk(t) = (xt−k+1 + … + xt) / k

Reading the signal

When the 20-day average sits above the 50-day average, short-term momentum is running ahead of the medium-term trend, and vice-versa. Watch for the two lines crossing.

Drill-down hierarchy

Year → Quarter → Month → Day, matching the DateTable hierarchy in the source report. Switching levels re-aggregates every chart and KPI on this page.

About this data. The supplied Power BI package stores its row-level table inside a compressed internal data model that could not be decoded into raw rows for this rebuild. To keep the dashboard fully interactive and self-contained, the series above is an illustrative reconstruction that follows the broad, well-known shape of NFLX's 2020–2022 trading history (pandemic-era run-up, 2021 peak, the Jan 2022 and Apr 2022 earnings-driven drawdowns). Treat exact daily figures as representative, not as verified market data.
